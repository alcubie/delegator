# Contributing

Contributions are welcome after prior discussion with the maintainers. Run
`make check` before submitting a change.

## Inbound license

By submitting a contribution, you license that contribution under
[FSL-1.1-ALv2](LICENSE), including its grant of the Apache License, Version 2.0
on the second anniversary of the date Alcubi makes the version containing the
contribution available. This is an inbound-equals-outbound policy: accepted
contributions are offered on the same terms as the rest of Delegator.

You must have the right to submit the contribution. A contribution must identify
third-party material and its license; do not submit material whose terms are
incompatible with Delegator's license or distribution.

Delegator does not currently require a contributor license agreement or a
Developer Certificate of Origin. Before accepting external contributions,
Alcubi must settle contributor governance if it wants to preserve the ability
to relicense those contributions unilaterally. This policy alone does not grant
that ability.
