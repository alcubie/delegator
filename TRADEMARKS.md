## Modified distributions and forks

A modified distribution or fork must have its own primary name and branding.
Unless you have written permission from Matthew McCormick, you must:

- remove Alcubi, Alcubi Delegator, and Delegator from the fork's product,
  service, package, archive, installer, website, account, and organization
  names;
- rename a modified binary so that it is not distributed as `dg`; and
- remove official logos, taglines, visual trade dress, and other
  source-identifying Alcubi branding.

You may still give accurate, reasonably prominent attribution without implying
endorsement. For example: "Northstar is a fork of Alcubi Delegator and is not
affiliated with or endorsed by Matthew McCormick." A link to the official
project may accompany that statement. Technical or historical identifiers that
must remain in copied source or interoperability documentation may remain when
they identify origin or compatibility rather than the fork itself.

Examples that require renaming or written permission include:

- publishing modified binaries under the name `dg`;
- offering "Alcubi Delegator Community Edition" or "Delegator Pro" as the name
  of a fork;
- naming a hosted fork or support service so that it appears to be the official
  Alcubi offering; and
- using official artwork or a confusingly similar visual identity for a fork.
